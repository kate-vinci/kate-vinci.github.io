PhD candidate – Artificial Intelligence – Embodied AI agents

  • CV
  • katerina.vinciguerra@unipr.it

I am a PhD candidate at the Department of Engineering and Architecture, University of Parma, Italy, supervised by Prof. Letizia Marchegiani. My research focuses on teaching machines, such as autonomous vehicles, to perceive (audio, video, etc.) and understand the world in order to plan and navigate through it.

I previously worked for three years as a data scientist at Schneider Electric, then at KPMG, after graduating with my master's degrees from Grenoble Ecole de Management and Grenoble INP (Ensimag).

Always happy to do research on topics of interest, feel free to reach out by email or on LinkedIn!
